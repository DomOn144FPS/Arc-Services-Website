Everything you need

Arc handles the backend so you can focus on building the game.

Services

Accounts
Player registration, login, and session management out of the box. Profiles are stored securely and work across platforms.
Matchmaking
Queue management and skill-based grouping built in. You define the rules and Arc handles the rest.
Parties
Let players group up and queue together. The party stays intact all the way through matchmaking.
Anti-cheat
Keeps your game fair by detecting cheats and suspicious behaviour on the player's machine. Arc handles flagging and ban management so you don't have to build it yourself.

Additional Features

Cloud Saves
Player progress saved to the cloud and synced across every device they play on. Saves can be restored at any time.
Leaderboards
Global rankings that update in real time as scores come in. No ranking infrastructure to build or maintain.
Telemetry
Send custom events from your game and see how players are actually playing. Useful for balance and design decisions.
Title Storage
Host game patches, config files, and other content on Arc's infrastructure. Push updates to players whenever you need to without managing your own file servers.
